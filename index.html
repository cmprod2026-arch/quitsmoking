Your privacy matters.

Welcome to Quit Smoking. We believe that your personal journey toward a smoke-free life should remain private.

Quit Smoking is designed to work locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on external servers.

✓ No personal data collection
01

Information Collection

Quit Smoking does not collect personal information.

We do not require account registration and do not request information such as:

  • Full name or email address
  • Phone number or home address
  • Precise location data
  • Personal identification details
  • Contacts or personal files

We do not operate a backend server that receives or stores your smoking-related information.

02

Local Data Storage

Quit Smoking stores the information required for its functionality locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Daily cigarette consumption records
  • Smoking history and calendar entries
  • Smoke-free day calculations
  • Progress and achievement information
  • Application preferences and settings

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

The information is used solely to provide the application's tracking and progress features.

03

How Your Information Is Used

Information stored locally by Quit Smoking is used only to provide the core features of the application.

  • Display daily smoking records
  • Track your smoke-free progress
  • Show your smoking history in the calendar
  • Calculate progress and milestones
  • Remember your preferences

We do not use your smoking information for advertising, profiling, marketing, or tracking.

04

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or transfer your personal information to third parties.

Since Quit Smoking does not collect or transmit your smoking records to external servers, we do not have access to your personal smoking history.

Your smoking progress and calendar entries remain under your control on your device.

05

Analytics and Tracking

Quit Smoking does not use its own analytics or tracking systems to monitor your behavior.

We do not collect your smoking history, usage patterns, or personal activity for analytical purposes.

Your smoking-related data is processed locally to display the application's features and statistics.

06

Data Security

Your smoking records and application settings are stored locally on your device.

We do not maintain an external database containing your smoking history or personal progress information.

The security of locally stored information also depends on the security of your device and operating system.

07

Data Retention and Deletion

Your smoking history and preferences are stored locally for as long as the application retains its local data.

You can remove locally stored application information by clearing the application's storage through your device settings.

Uninstalling the application typically removes its locally stored data, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.

Since we do not store your smoking information on our servers, there is no corresponding server-side smoking history for us to delete.

08

Health Information

Quit Smoking is intended to help users monitor their smoking habits and stay motivated to reduce or stop smoking.

Any smoking-related information entered into the application is processed locally on your device.

Statistics and progress indicators are provided for informational and motivational purposes only.

Quit Smoking is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

09

Children's Privacy

Quit Smoking is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

The application is intended to support people who wish to reduce or stop smoking.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in application functionality or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

We encourage users to review this policy periodically.
