Privacy Policy

Your journey to a smoke-free life matters. Your privacy matters too.

Last updated: November 14, 2026
Welcome to Quit Smoking. We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is handled when you use our application. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and how it is protected.

01 Information We Collect

Quit Smoking does not require account registration and does not directly request personal identification information such as your full name, email address, phone number, or home address.

The application may process information you provide to support your smoking cessation journey, including:

  • Smoking habits and cigarette consumption
  • Your selected quit date
  • Smoking cessation progress and milestones
  • Estimated money saved and cigarettes avoided
  • Personal preferences and app settings

Third-party services integrated into the application may also automatically collect limited technical information, such as:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Basic application interaction data
  • Device type and operating system information

02 How Information Is Used

Information processed by Quit Smoking may be used to:

  • Track your smoke-free progress
  • Calculate estimated health and financial milestones
  • Provide motivational statistics and achievements
  • Remember your application preferences
  • Maintain and improve app functionality
  • Support advertising and purchase functionality

Information related to smoking cessation is intended to support the app's tracking and motivational features.

03 Advertising

Quit Smoking may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners, including Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may collect and process certain technical information to:

  • Display advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Improve advertising services

Learn more about Google's advertising technologies and privacy practices:

Google Advertising Privacy Policy

04 In-App Purchases

Quit Smoking may offer optional in-app purchases, such as removing advertisements or unlocking premium functionality.

Purchases made through Google Play are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not directly collect or store payment card details.

Payment information is handled by the relevant payment platform according to its own privacy and security policies.

05 Data Storage and Security

Quit Smoking may store application preferences and smoking cessation progress on your device to provide tracking and motivational features.

We aim to protect information through appropriate security practices and trusted third-party service providers.

Where supported, communications with third-party services use encrypted connections such as HTTPS/TLS.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

06 Health Information

Quit Smoking is designed to help users monitor their smoking cessation progress and stay motivated.

Information about smoking habits, quit dates, and progress may be considered health-related information.

Health progress estimates and statistics displayed in the application are for general informational and motivational purposes only.

The application does not provide professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

07 Children's Privacy

Quit Smoking is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

08 Third-Party Services

Quit Smoking may use third-party services to support application functionality, advertising, and purchases.

These services may include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Billing

Third-party providers operate under their own privacy policies. We encourage users to review those policies for more information about their data practices.

09 Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your device settings and the features available in the application, you may be able to:

  • Manage advertising preferences in your device settings
  • Reset or restrict your advertising identifier
  • Manage application permissions
  • Remove locally stored application data
  • Uninstall the application at any time

You may also contact us with questions regarding your information or privacy rights.

10 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application, applicable requirements, or third-party services.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
